Mapping Technology to Global Goals

Aegis Solution is designed as a human-centric industrial safety platform aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Goal 3: Good Health & Well-being

Objective: Ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all, especially for workers operating in hazardous and extreme environments.

Target 3.4

Reduce premature mortality from non-communicable diseases.

Mapping: Continuous ECG monitoring to prevent sudden cardiac incidents.

Target 3.8

Achieve access to quality health services.

Mapping: Extending health monitoring to isolated zones without hospital access.

Target 3.9

Reduce illnesses from hazardous environments.

Mapping: Detecting hypothermia risk and thermal stress in real-time.

Goal 8: Decent Work & Economic Growth

Objective: Promote safe, secure, and productive working environments while supporting economic growth.

Target 8.8

Protect labor rights and promote safe environments.

Mapping: Automated alert system reducing dependence on manual checks.

Target 8.2

Achieve higher economic productivity.

Mapping: Reduced downtime from health incidents and data-driven safety.

Target 8.1

Sustain economic growth.

Mapping: Affordable safety solution tailored for MSMEs.

Goal 9: Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure

Objective: Build resilient infrastructure and promote inclusive, sustainable industrial innovation.

Target 9.4

Upgrade infrastructure to be sustainable.

Mapping: Offline-first architecture working in harsh, low-connectivity zones.

Target 9.5

Enhance scientific research and innovation.

Mapping: Research-driven design integrating ECG, IMU, and edge AI.

Target 9.b

Support domestic technology development.

Mapping: Indigenous hardware/software development focused on local needs.

Measurable Social & Industrial Impact

  • Reduced response time during medical emergencies
  • Improved worker safety compliance
  • Lower accident-related downtime
  • Increased confidence among workers
  • Affordable access to advanced safety systems

Aegis Solution supports the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals through technology-driven industrial safety and worker well-being.
