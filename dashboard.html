Dashboard Features

Device & Worker Management

The dashboard supports dynamic employee assignment to wearable devices.

  • Device Selection: Shows connected status and hardware ID.
  • Employee Profiles: Manage name, age, blood group, and contact info.
  • Quick Onboarding: New employees can be added securely without downtime.

Continuous Monitoring

Time-series graphs for Heart Rate, Temperature, Respiratory Rate, and Blood Pressure to observe trends and detect anomalies.

Health Alert System

Edge-based logic triggers alerts (Green/Yellow/Red) instantly without cloud dependency, ensuring safety during network failures.

Data Logging

Maintains time-stamped health readings and alert history for safety audits, compliance reporting, and incident analysis.

Real-time Alerts

Immediate notifications for falls, abnormal vitals, or geofence breaches. Configurable thresholds for different work zones.

Historical Analytics

Review shift patterns, exposure times, and health trends to optimize worker schedules and prevent fatigue.

Deep Dive Analytics

Comprehensive historical data view for post-shift analysis and compliance auditing using Delphi-TVS standards.

Trust & Reliability

This dashboard represents a working proof-of-concept built using real sensor data, edge processing, and industrial communication protocols. It is designed as a foundation for deployable, scalable, and compliant industrial safety systems.
