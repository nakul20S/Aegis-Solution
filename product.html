Aegis Wearable Safety System

An IoT-based, edge-intelligent health monitoring solution designed to protect industrial workers operating in extreme, sealed, and hazardous environments.

The Problem We Solve

In many industrial settings, especially cold chambers (–20 °C) and sealed zones, workers operate with minimal supervision. Traditional safety methods are reactive and fail to detect emergencies in time.

Critical Risks:

  • Hypothermia and fatigue due to extreme cold
  • Sudden cardiac events or fainting going unnoticed
  • Falls or prolonged immobility in isolated chambers
  • Delayed response due to manual checks

Our Solution Approach

A multi-tier, wearable-centric safety architecture that shifts industrial safety from reactive monitoring to proactive prevention.

  1. Wearable Safety Band
  2. Central Hub (Inside Zone)
  3. Main Monitoring Hub (Outside Zone)

Core Product Components

1. Wearable Safety Band

A lightweight, non-intrusive armband designed for continuous industrial use.

  • ECG-based heart rate monitoring (medical-grade accuracy)
  • Body temperature monitoring with high precision
  • Motion and fall detection using IMU sensing
  • Low-power operation for full-shift monitoring
  • Designed to operate reliably in sub-zero environments

2. Central Hub (Inside the Chamber)

The central hub acts as the local intelligence layer inside the hazardous environment.

  • Receives real-time data from multiple wearables
  • Performs edge-level analysis to detect abnormal conditions
  • Integrates secondary presence and motion verification
  • Forwards consolidated data via a robust wired link

3. Main Monitoring & Alert Hub

Located outside the hazardous zone, this hub ensures clear and immediate human awareness.

Green Light: Worker condition normal

Yellow Light: Warning (temp drop, stillness)

Red Light + Buzzer: Emergency (fall, critical vitals)

Reliable Communication Architecture

Engineered for environments where standard wireless systems fail.

  • Wireless low-latency communication between wearable and central hub
  • Industrial-grade wired communication between internal and external hubs
  • Immune to metal shielding, EMI noise, and sealed-chamber constraints

Live Dashboard & Monitoring

Supervisors can access a web-based dashboard that provides:

  • Vital parameter trends
  • Alert history and event logs
  • Multi-worker and multi-zone monitoring

Research & Validation

Developed within the Live-In-Labs program at Sri Sairam Engineering College, Aegis has undergone rigorous testing to ensure reliability in hostile environments.

Extreme Cold Testing

The system has been validated for continuous operation in sub-zero conditions, a specific requirement for blast freezers and cold storage units.

  • Test Environment: Industrial Deep Freezer Simulation
  • Temperature: sustained operation at -20 °C
  • Duration: 8-hour shift cycles
  • Outcome: 100% data transmission success rate with no battery degradation

Signal Penetration

Overcoming the Faraday cage effect of sealed metal chambers.

  • Challenge: Thick insulated metal walls block Wi-Fi/Bluetooth.
  • Solution: Hybrid Wired-Wireless Mesh.
  • Latency: < 200ms for alert triggering.
  • Redundancy: Local buzzer activation even if main server link fails.
